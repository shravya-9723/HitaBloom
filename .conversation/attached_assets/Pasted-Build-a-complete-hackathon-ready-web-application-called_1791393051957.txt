Build a complete hackathon-ready web application called HitaBloom.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a generic healthcare dashboard.
It should feel like a beautiful, playful, gamified health journey similar in emotional engagement to Snapchat streaks, combined with an illustrated adventure world, AI talking characters, cultural wellness, and doctor-supervised care.

The visual design should follow the attached HitaBloom storyboard/image as the primary UI/UX reference.

DO NOT build a 3D game.
DO NOT build a geographic map.
The Patient World should be a large, beautiful 2D illustrated health world/home screen with connected locations, gardens, paths, characters and progression.

The application must feel polished enough for a hackathon demo.

==================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA
==================================================

HitaBloom is an AI-powered health world where patients:

1. Choose their language.
2. Tell HitaBloom what they are feeling in natural language.
3. AI identifies the relevant health/body area for educational purposes.
4. A friendly talking body character explains the body system and the symptom/topic generally.
5. HitaBloom provides contextual Cultural Wellness information.
6. A doctor provides the actual patient-specific care instructions.
7. AI explains the doctor's instructions in the patient's selected language without changing them.
8. AI suggests a personalized engagement quest.
9. Doctor approves the quest.
10. Patient completes the quest.
11. Patient earns XP, maintains a health streak and receives character celebration.
12. The corresponding garden/world area blooms.
13. The next area unlocks.
14. Patient returns to the HitaBloom World and continues the journey.

Core loop:

SYMPTOM
→ AI UNDERSTANDING
→ BODY CHARACTER
→ CULTURAL WELLNESS
→ DOCTOR CARE
→ AI EXPLANATION
→ QUEST
→ DOCTOR APPROVAL
→ COMPLETE QUEST
→ XP + STREAK
→ GARDEN BLOOMS
→ NEXT AREA UNLOCKS
